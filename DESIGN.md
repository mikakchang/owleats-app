## Visual language

- **Color roles:** deep teal for action and trust, cream for the ambient canvas, white paper for content surfaces, mint for positive routing, saffron for events and time, and coral for live food/special signals.
- **Typography:** DM Sans handles navigation, labels, controls, and menu content. Newsreader is reserved for the human, editorial moments: the hero emphasis, event title, and setup heading.
- **Composition:** generous open space above the decision surface; no generic hero card. A small orbit illustration echoes a campus map and establishes the “worth the walk” idea without requiring photography.
- **Interaction:** meal period, servery, dietary lens, and preferences all update the demo immediately. The SMS preview mirrors the selected context and stays visible beside the primary menu.
- **Responsive behavior:** the side column stacks beneath the menu on tablet/mobile; the orbit recedes behind the copy; filters remain horizontally scrollable; the preference modal remains keyboard and touch-friendly.
